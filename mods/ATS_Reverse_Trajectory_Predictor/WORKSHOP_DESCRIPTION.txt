[h1]Reverse Posture Assistant For ATS V1.62.2.2[/h1]
[b]American Truck Simulator · ATS 1.62.2.2 · Windows x64[/b]

World-space reverse prediction, blue tractor and orange trailer boundaries, 5 m prediction distance. Road slope follows grounded wheel centres and suspension travel. Lines appear in reverse gear and hide while paused.
